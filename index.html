Playbook

The path every clip takes, and the rules it has to pass. Shaded steps are yours.

FindTop Twitch clips from the last 24 h
PickScore the moment, 70+ gets cut
Edit15–30 s, captions, zooms, sound
CheckAuto QC, then the clip reviewer

PostPublic, 10+ min apart
SubmitContent Rewards within 30 min

Every clip

  • 15–30 s, starts on the action, ends on the payoff
  • One-word captions, 1–3 tags, 1–2 zooms, credit
  • Campaign clips: "Paid partnership" on screen, #ad in the description
  • Unpaid clips: no disclosure, never submitted later
  • QC passes and the clip reviewer says PASS

Posting

  • Public right away, never unlisted first
  • Never two uploads within 10 minutes
  • Submit on Content Rewards within 30 minutes
  • Same clip never on both channels
  • Stalled channel: 1 clip a day until it recovers

Required tags

  • Lacy: "Lacy" + #LACY
  • Clix: "Clix" + #Clix
  • Kai Cenat: "Kai Cenat" + #KaiCenat
  • YourRAGE: #yourrage

Hard skips

  • Nudity (NPCs too), sexual talk or held shots
  • Jokes about real tragedies
  • Real-world violence, minors, personal info
  • Slurs and sexual words are muted; other swears only kept at the peak
  • GTA crime, cops and chaos are fine
